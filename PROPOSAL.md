## what I am building
I want to build a web tool that shows every printers status. 
## who it is for
it is for every babson student
## what it remember 
For each of the 12 printers:

Its name (e.g. “Printer 3”) and model
Its current state: free, printing, done (waiting for pickup), paused, failed, or offline
Print progress (%)
Estimated time left

For the whole page:

When the data was last updated, so students know if it’s fresh
A count of how many printers are free right now
It does not remember file names, who started the print, or camera images, to protect student privacy.
